Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to talk to you and do your project: your name, email and profile picture from Google when you sign in, and whatever you write to us. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Stydio ([registered business name and type, e.g. "a partnership firm"]) is a branding and web design studio run by Anay Injnani and Mitali S Rao, based at [full postal address], India ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

3. How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries, manage your project and send you updates about it.
  • To send quotes and invoices, and to keep records required by tax and accounting law.
  • To keep the website and client area secure and working.
  • To show finished work in our portfolio, but only with your permission (see our Terms).

We use your data on the basis of your consent (which you give when you sign in and send us a request) and, for invoices and tax records, because the law requires it. We do not send marketing emails unless you separately agree to them.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the services that run this website, each under its own privacy and security terms:

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase: our database and login system. Your account and project data are stored in its Mumbai, India region.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts: deliver code libraries and fonts to your browser, which means they see your IP address.
  • Razorpay (once online payments are enabled): processes payments.

We may also disclose data if the law or a court order requires it.

5. Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. When you sign in, your browser stores a login token in local storage so you stay signed in, and we briefly keep an unsent form in session storage while you sign in. Signing out removes the login token.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and project conversations: while you're a client, and up to 3 years after your last project, so we can support work we delivered.
  • Invoices and payment records: 8 years, as required by Indian tax law.
  • Enquiries that never became a project: up to 12 months.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • withdraw consent and ask us to delete it (except records we must keep by law);
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you're not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Email support.stydio@gmail.com from the email you signed in with. We'll reply within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Every table in our database is protected so each client can only see their own projects, and only the two of us can see everyone's. No security is perfect; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us instead.
