Refunds & Cancellations

Effective 1 November 2026 · Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: cancel before we start and you get your advance back. Once work has started, you pay for the work done and we refund the rest of what you've paid. Approved refunds reach you within 5–7 working days.

1. Cancelling a project

When you cancelWhat happens
Before work starts (within 48 hours of paying the advance, and before our kickoff call)Full refund of the advance.
After work has startedWe charge for the work completed so far, based on the stages in your quote, and refund any amount you've paid above that. The minimum charge is 25% of the project fee.
After final delivery or launchNo refund. The work has been delivered and approved.

To cancel, write to us at support.stydio@gmail.com or post in your project in My projects. The cancellation date is the day we receive your message.

2. If we cancel

If we have to end a project for reasons on our side, we'll refund everything you've paid for work not yet delivered, and hand over everything completed so far.

3. Not happy with the work?

Each project includes revision rounds so we can get it right. Tell us what isn't working and we'll revise it within the agreed scope. If we still can't meet the brief in your quote, we'll agree a fair partial refund with you.

4. What we can't refund

  • Third-party costs already paid on your behalf (domains, hosting, paid fonts, stock images, plugins).
  • Payment gateway or bank charges.
  • Extra work you approved outside the original quote, once delivered.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (bank account, UPI or card) within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. We'll confirm the refund amount in writing before we send it.

6. Failed or duplicate payments

If money left your account but the payment failed, or you were charged twice, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or our payment provider within 5–7 working days. If not, contact us with the payment ID and we'll sort it out.

7. Contact

Questions about a refund: support.stydio@gmail.com, phone / WhatsApp +91 86691 80693.
