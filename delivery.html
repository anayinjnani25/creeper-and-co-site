Delivery Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: everything we make is digital, so nothing is shipped. We deliver files and websites online, on the timeline in your quote, and you can follow every step in My projects.

1. What we deliver

Our services are digital: brand identities, design files, brand guidelines, websites, code and 3D or motion work. We don't sell or ship physical products. If a project includes printed items, they are produced and shipped by a printer you choose or approve, under that printer's own terms.

2. How you receive it

  • Design files and guidelines: a secure download link (for example Google Drive), shared in My projects or by email.
  • Websites: published to your domain and hosting, or handed over as a code repository you own.
  • Updates and previews: posted in your project in My projects as work progresses.

3. When you receive it

Every quote includes a timeline. As a guide:

ProjectTypical timeline
Website (up to 5 pages)2–3 weeks
Brand identity + website4–6 weeks
Full brand identity with strategy6–8 weeks

The timeline starts when we receive the advance and everything we need from you. Waiting for content, feedback or approvals moves the delivery date by the same amount. We'll tell you straight away if anything on our side changes the date.

4. Final handover

Final files, admin access and ownership are handed over once the final payment is received (see Terms of Service). We'll confirm in writing when handover is complete.

5. Problems with a delivery

If a link doesn't work or a file is missing, tell us in My projects or at hello@creeperand.co [confirm address] and we'll resend it within one working day.
